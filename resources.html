Finding support

You matter. Help is here.

You do not need to have the right words to reach out. Support is available.

988Call or text

In the United States

Call or text 988 to connect with the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. The service is free and available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. You can reach out about emotional distress even if you are not thinking about suicide.

Call 988Text 988Visit the 988 Lifeline ↗

If someone is in immediate danger

Call 911 or your local emergency number. If possible, stay with the person while you seek immediate help.

Spirit of Scotty shares education and resources but is not a crisis response service, healthcare provider, or emergency contact.

For someone you love

Listening without judgment and helping someone find support can make it easier to have a difficult conversation. You do not have to figure everything out on your own.

For practical guidance, explore the 988 Lifeline resources for helping others ↗.

Need someone to talk to?

In the U.S., call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If there is immediate danger, call 911.
